Providers' requests

What each data provider asks of anyone using its data, quoted from the provider's own pages: the works to cite, and the notices to carry with the data. Every file the package fetches records the same in its provenance (see Provenance), with a link to its section here. Where a text holds DD-MMM-YYYY or YYYY, a record fills in the date or the year the file was fetched; here they are shown as the provider writes them.

A notice is required where the provider or the data's licence says it must accompany the data, requested where the provider asks for it, and optional where the provider offers it for those who wish to use it.

The page is made from the files in data/catalogue/requests/, a section for each.
